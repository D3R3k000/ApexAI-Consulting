Governance & Architecture Principles

Designed around the risks operations leaders actually care about.

PRINCIPLE 01

Human-in-the-Loop by Default

We never put an unvetted language model directly in front of paying customers or production IT permissions. Systems start in assist-mode—triaging, summarizing, and drafting—until accuracy is proven over hundreds of real tickets.

PRINCIPLE 02

Grounded Strictly in Your Docs

Responses are constrained to your verified help center articles, policy documents, and IT runbooks. When documentation is missing or confidence falls below threshold, the workflow explicitly defers to a human instead of guessing.

PRINCIPLE 03

Full Client Ownership

Everything is deployed inside your own cloud, helpdesk, and automation accounts. You retain full ownership of all prompts, routing logic, documentation indexes, and API keys—with zero proprietary vendor lock-in.
